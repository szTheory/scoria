Scoria logo options — gate #2

Six hand-tuned marks (each with a combination lockup) across three directions, plus two integrated typemark studies. Every option is shown on both grounds at 256 / 64 / 32 / 16 px, in monochrome, as a browser-tab favicon, a 24 px sidebar, and a README header band. Pick a direction — or send it back for a second round.

Standalone file:// page · inline SVG · no network · IBM Plex Sans system-fallback.

Recommended: TV-1 — Trace Vesicle "Span rail"

  1. #1 Primary — TV-1 "Span rail" (Trace Vesicle). The trace-tree hole hierarchy (root → child → leaf) gives this mark genuine internal logic that reads as deliberate structure, not random porosity — surviving monochrome where the Cinder options read as generic porous rock. Its 3-hole favicon simplification is the only one that keeps each hole ≥1.5px at 16px with surviving webs (geometry-verified), the tight ~1:1 silhouette fills the sidebar and favicon slots without starving either, and its internal trace logic is orthogonal to Threadline's line motif and distinct from every devtools peer (abstract nodes / colorful blobs).
    • TV-2 "Open branch" — close runner-up. Edge-notch breaks the closed-blob feel, matching the "somewhat breaking the boundaries" taste, at a small cost to favicon tidiness. Advance this if TV-1 feels too quiet.
    • TV-3 "Cross rail" — third Trace Vesicle; horizontal reading of the span tree. A valid alternative if the vertical layout reads as too narrow.
  2. #2 Fallback — CM-1 "Field sample" or CM-2 "Tumbled" (Cinder). Simpler geometry = excellent favicon survival and lower visual complexity. Pick this direction if the Trace Vesicle feels too busy at small sizes or if the trace-tree concept reads as too literal. CM-1 has a dominant core; CM-2 is softer with higher porosity.
  3. #3 Study only — AP-1 "Aperture". Included so the camera/eye risk is visible and rejectable with evidence. The central cavity reads too much as a lens or aperture blade. Not recommended as the primary direction.
  4. Integrated typemarks — TYPE-1 "Ring o" / TYPE-2 "Porous a". First-class variants for narrow contexts, not fallbacks. Wanted alongside whichever mark direction is chosen.

My pick: TV-1 "Span rail." It is the only option that passes every hard test cleanly — its 16px favicon keeps 3 well-separated holes (each ≥1.5px rendered, verified by geometry), it reads as a deliberate trace hierarchy in monochrome, and its tight ~1:1 silhouette never starves the sidebar/favicon slots. TV-2 is the close runner-up if you want more energy from the edge-notch. Cinder (CM-1 / CM-2) is the honest #2 if trace-tree complexity is a concern.

None of these? That is a valid answer. Choosing "none — second round" sends the direction back for another iteration with your notes, rather than forcing a compromise pick.

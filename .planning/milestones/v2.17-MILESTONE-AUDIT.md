BRAND-09 fires only if Phase 18's pressure-test audit found material contrast/accessibility/coherence failures requiring propagation into `assets/css/02-tokens.css`. The Phase 18 WCAG audit ran all 52 token pairings (22 documented + 30 shipped) through the contrast checker and found **0 FAIL** results. Gate #1 recorded `propagation: not-required` in a fenced machine-readable block at line 1290 of `brandbook/pressure-test.md`. BRAND-09's `[ ]` checkbox is the correct terminal state. `test/support/ds06_baseline.txt` and `assets/css/02-tokens.css` are byte-identical to their pre-milestone state.

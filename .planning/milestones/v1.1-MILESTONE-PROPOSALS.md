# Scoria: Next Milestone Proposals (v1.1 / v2.0)

Based on the core vision of **Scoria** as the Phoenix-native AI ops layer (tracing, evaluating, governing) and the completion of the v1.0 MVP (Observability, MCP Gateway, Operator UX, Eval Flywheel), here is a deep synthesis of three "meaty" paths forward. 

Each option is designed to be cohesive with the "Field Engineer" brand, the "Batteries-Included but Composable" DNA, and idiomatic Elixir/OTP architecture.

---

## Option 1: The "Caldera" Milestone — Durable Agent Workflows & Handoffs

**Theme:** Evolving from single-turn chat & tool calls to durable, stateful, multi-agent workflows with human-in-the-loop (HITL) checkpoints.

**Core Objective:** Build a lightweight, OTP-native agent loop capable of managing complex state machines, subagent handoffs, and durable pauses for long-running workflows or operator approval.

**Key Features:**
- **Durable Checkpoints:** Persist the `RunState` into Ecto natively so workflows can survive BEAM restarts, timeout limits, and operator approval delays.
- **Handoffs & Subagents:** First-class support for an agent to cleanly delegate to another `MyAI.Agent` with specialized tools.
- **Jido Integration (Optional):** Provide an adapter to map `Jido` v2 pure-functional decisions/directives into Scoria's observable trace and checkpoint engine.
- **LiveView Workflow Visualizer:** A visual representation of the agent graph/state machine in the admin dashboard.

**Pros, Cons & Tradeoffs:**
- *Pros:* Captures the highly demanded "Agentic Workflow" use cases (similar to LangGraph). Deeply leverages Elixir/OTP's strengths (concurrency, supervision) for robust execution.
- *Cons:* Extremely high risk of over-engineering or making the agent "magical" and un-debuggable. 
- *Idiomatic Approach:* Separate the "Plan" (pure functional data transformation, testable) from the "Action" (side-effecting directives). Use GenServers (or Oban) to execute the workflow steps, emitting telemetry events at every transition.
- *Lessons Learned:* LangGraph succeeded by emphasizing durable execution and debugging visibility for complex stateful workflows. We must avoid black-box execution; every step must map to a visible `Trace` and `Span`.

---

## Option 2: The "Corpus" Milestone — Advanced RAG, Citations & Knowledge Grounding

**Theme:** Elevating Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to a first-class, observable, and evaluable operation within Scoria.

**Core Objective:** Provide batteries-included RAG primitives that natively integrate with the Scoria trace tree and Eval Workbench.

**Key Features:**
- **Vector Index & Chunking Abstractions:** Clean behaviors for embedding models and chunking strategies.
- **pgvector Integration:** An out-of-the-box Ecto/pgvector adapter to store and query embeddings without needing an external vector database.
- **First-Class Citations:** Ensure the trace explicitly captures "Retrieval" spans, linking the model's generated output back to specific `Chunks` via `Citation` metadata.
- **Groundedness & Hallucination Scorers:** Add specific deterministic and LLM-as-Judge scorers to the Eval Workbench that verify if the output is factually grounded in the retrieved context.

**Pros, Cons & Tradeoffs:**
- *Pros:* Addresses the most common enterprise use case (RAG). Highly visible "Trust Builders" (citations, evidence) directly align with the "Grounded, evidence-based" brand archetype.
- *Cons:* Vector similarity search is a crowded space; we must avoid building "just another vector wrapper" and focus strictly on the *observability and evaluation* of the retrieval.
- *Idiomatic Approach:* Leverage `pgvector` inside the existing Ecto Repo to keep the "batteries-included" promise simple (`mix scoria.install`).
- *Lessons Learned:* RAG often fails silently. The ecosystem lesson is that you need *metrics* (Hit Rate, MRR) and *evals* (Groundedness) tied directly to the retrieval span, not just the final text. 

---

## Option 3: The "Seismograph" Milestone — SRE, Circuit Breakers & Ecosystem Synergy

**Theme:** Hardening Scoria into a true production-grade control plane with proactive governance, circuit breakers, and deep `szTheory` ecosystem integration.

**Core Objective:** Move from passive observability (looking at traces) to active governance and alerting (acting on thresholds).

**Key Features:**
- **Circuit Breakers & Budgets:** Introduce configurable token and cost budgets per tenant/user. If a budget is exceeded, Scoria natively trips a guardrail.
- **Parapet SLO Integration:** Define Service Level Objectives (SLOs) for latency, cost, and eval pass rates, exposing hooks intended for the upcoming `Parapet` SRE layer.
- **Threadline Audit Export:** Clean integration to export sensitive `ToolApproval` and MCP access events into immutable audit logs.
- **Automated Regression Alerts:** When a CI gate or online eval dips below the baseline, automatically trigger incident workflows (via `Chimeway`/`Mailglass`).

**Pros, Cons & Tradeoffs:**
- *Pros:* Cements Scoria as the undisputed "Operator-Grade" choice. This delivers immense value to Solo SaaS founders who need safety nets for AI costs and quality.
- *Cons:* Focuses heavily on the "Ops" side and less on new "AI capabilities," which might feel less exciting to some developers.
- *Idiomatic Approach:* Heavy reliance on `telemetry` event attachment and background `Oban` queues to process asynchronous evaluations and cost aggregations without blocking the hot path.
- *Lessons Learned:* Uncapped LLM loops can bankrupt solo developers overnight. Proactive circuit breakers and budget tracking are the #1 missing feature in most lightweight LLM wrappers.

---

## Strategic Recommendation

Given the overarching goal of moving the Scoria vision rapidly forward and focusing on meaty, differentiating features, **Option 1 ("Caldera" - Durable Agent Workflows)** provides the largest architectural leap forward. It brings OTP-native state machines and handoffs to the table, solving the hardest problem in AI engineering right now (durable multi-step reasoning) while staying deeply aligned with Scoria's tracing and evaluation core. 

However, if your immediate application demands secure knowledge extraction, **Option 2 (RAG)** provides immediate end-user value. If cost and safety are the immediate bottlenecks to going live, **Option 3 (SRE/Budgets)** is the prerequisite.

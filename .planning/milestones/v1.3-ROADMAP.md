**Status:** ✅ SHIPPED 2026-05-12

## Overview

Phases 7 through 11 hardened Scoria into a production-grade SRE control plane. The milestone ships durable budget reservations and reconciliation, external-effect circuit breakers, audited approval lineage, incident-to-delivery routing, runtime and incident telemetry, and a repaired milestone evidence chain backed by fresh verification.

## Milestone Summary

**Key Decisions:**

- Keep Seismograph behind `Scoria.SRE`, `Scoria.Workflows`, and existing LiveView surfaces instead of creating a separate SRE product layer.
- Treat breaker-open exits as reconciled budget outcomes with explicit evidence, not as accounting exceptions.
- Route approval truth and audit lineage through workflow-owned boundaries only.
- Split ordinary verification from pgvector-backed knowledge verification so the default lane remains boring and installable.

**Issues Resolved:**

- Closed the reserve -> breaker-open -> reconcile gap at both workflow and MCP seams.
- Restored audited approval handling and durable notification delivery production on the real incident path.
- Wired canonical runtime and incident telemetry into live execution paths.
- Repaired the milestone evidence chain by adding the missing Phase 7 verification artifact and aligning shipped-state files.

**Issues Deferred:**

- `SEED-001-agentcore-lessons` remains dormant for a future milestone and was explicitly acknowledged at close.

**Technical Debt:**

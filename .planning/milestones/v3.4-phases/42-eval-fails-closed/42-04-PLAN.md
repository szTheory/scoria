<objective>
Make the offline replay runner score real subject output through a real scorer and derive its verdict from the shared `Verdict` (D-03 dispatch + D-04 kill-the-hardcoded-pass). Delete the `record_scores/4` fake pass and route each item by `scorer_kind`.

Purpose: `Runner.run_offline` currently stamps `status: "passed", score: 1.0` on every item regardless of the subject output — the single largest fake-green site. After this plan, offline replay compares the frozen capture against the sealed expectation via `ExactMatch`, marks unknown scorers `:not_scored`, and computes the run verdict once via `Verdict.compute/2`.
Output: real per-item dispatch in `runner.ex` and a rewritten `offline_runner_test.exs` proving honest verdicts without any live LLM.
</objective>

### How to review

1. **Open `brandbook/tools/final-variants.html` in a browser.** Review all 8 variants on the dark (#11100F) and light (#FAF5EF) grounds at realistic sizes.
2. Check the favicon tile — it shows 16px AND 32px side-by-side; holes should read clearly at both sizes.
3. On the dark-ground section, note the mark-as-'o': the ember porous cinder sits in the x-height band of "Scoria"; its holes are the counter; the two-tone ember `o` is the brand's accent. On the light-ground section it uses Scoria-600 (#B94F31) for the accent.
4. Read `brandbook/tools/variant-spec.md` for the clear-space / min-size rules and the optical-pass rationale.

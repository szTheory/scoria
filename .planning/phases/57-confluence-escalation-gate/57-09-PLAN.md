<objective>
Make the human in the loop an informed one, give them a bounded way to grant an ongoing decision, and stop the reviewer surface from degrading under the volume this phase creates.

Purpose: the shipped reviewer drawer is routed, functional and correctly attributes the decision actor, and an unknown blocker kind does not crash it — but its rows are fixed at a target and a policy reason, with no row for the combination, the three legs, the grade, or which earlier step introduced the untrusted content. A human asked to authorize an exfiltration with no evidence of why it is one is a rubber stamp. This plan ships that evidence, the bounded approval scope the developer selected for D-50, and the pending-query cap.

It also pins the enum-to-string mapping HERE, in the phase that owns the enum, which is the specific mechanism preventing "Phase 58 cannot be built without a breaking change".

Output: confluence evidence rows, the named-combination label and tone mapping with its test, a scoped-approve action, and a capped pending query.
</objective>

<objective>
Give the human in the loop the evidence the reviewer drawer was already built to show, and prove it against a real escalation rather than a fixture.

Purpose: `ScoriaWeb.ApprovalCopy.confluence_rows/1` is correct and unit-tested, but its data source is never populated. `Scoria.Workflows.RemoteApprovalProjection.project_approval/1` never sets `:combination`, `:grade`, `:private_data_source`, `:untrusted_content_source` or `:exfil_source`, so on a real `Scoria.MCP.Executor` escalation those rows are silently dropped by `reject_blank_rows/1` and the reviewer sees only the target and a free-text policy reason. That is mitigated today only because the shipped default escalates on the strongest `declared` grade; under `strict: true` a weak-grade escalation becomes visually indistinguishable from a high-confidence one. This is acknowledged incomplete wiring, not an accepted limitation.

Output: the escalation-time evidence read in the projection, an end-to-end test that drives a real `Executor.execute/4` escalation through to the rendered rows, and the same proof asserted at the LiveView layer.
</objective>

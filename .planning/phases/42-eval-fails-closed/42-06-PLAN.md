<objective>
Turn online scoring into an honest negative-signal detector (D-06). Today `deterministic_scores/3` fabricates `passed/1.0` for any non-`policy_trigger` trace and `threshold_verdict/1` returns `"passed"` for an empty list — laundering fake labels toward golden datasets.

Purpose: A reference-free deterministic check can only honestly prove a NEGATIVE. This plan makes it emit `failed` for real negative signals (policy_trigger, span ERROR, empty output) and `[]` for a clean trace, leaving the LLM judge (when configured) as the only producer of a positive verdict — otherwise `needs_review`, never `promotion_candidate`.
Output: `online_scoring.ex` rewritten as a negative-signal detector with the span/step signal loads and Verdict wiring.
</objective>

<objective>
Give dataset items a frozen, hashable subject output (`captured_output`) populated at promotion, and add the single `SubjectOutput.resolve/2` resolver both runners will call (D-04). This replaces the two self-grade sites with a replay-from-capture cassette instead of a live trace re-read.

Purpose: A sealed eval must be self-contained and deterministic — replaying a frozen capture is key-free and cannot silently change or vanish the way a mutable/redactable trace body can. Empty capture resolves to :not_scored so the mass re-verdict of legacy empty-capture datasets is honest fail-closed behavior, not fake-green.
Output: schema fields + migration, promotion-time capture population, and `Scoria.Eval.SubjectOutput`.
</objective>

<objective>
Make the third rail real, and make its arithmetic correct by construction rather than by convention.

`max_active_ms` bounds *active* time — elapsed since the run's anchor minus accumulated paused time —
not wall clock. That distinction is the whole point: under wall clock, Phase 57's confluence gate (a
safety mechanism) becomes a liability, because every escalation a human does not answer in time
converts into a halted run and adopters learn to disable the gate to keep their timeouts usable.

The accumulator that makes it work is derived inside `Run.changeset/2` from the `:status` transition
itself, not written at call sites — because the call-site convention leaks. `complete_step/3` reads
the run with no regard for its current status and writes `run_status` unconditionally, so a sibling
completing while another step is parked at approval would flip the run back to `"running"` with
`rail_paused_at` still set, losing the interval forever and silently reverting the rail to wall clock.

Purpose: RAIL-01's `timeout` rail.
Output: changeset-derived pause accounting, the active-time predicate, and the invariant Phase 57
inherits.
</objective>

<objective>
Make `max_tool_calls` real rather than decorative, and prove the CAS actually holds under concurrency.

Today `max_tool_calls` would have ZERO coverage on every shipped path: `MCP.Executor` never loads a
`%Run{}` and `canonical_context/1` never derives `:run_id`; the three in-repo callers of
`Executor.execute/*` pass `Identity.to_map/1` (no `run_id`), `Map.new(conn.assigns)` (no `run_id`),
and `Invocation.invoke/4` (zero non-test callers). This plan enforces at both tool entry points,
injects the ids Scoria can supply, and telemeters the residual gap honestly instead of papering over
a total one.

Purpose: RAIL-01's tool-call rail plus success criterion #4 (no-run-attribution is an explicit,
telemetried no-op).
Output: `admit_tool_call/2`, both enforcement entry points, the `:skipped` event, the `run_id`
forward, and the concurrency/counter-semantics test battery.
</objective>
